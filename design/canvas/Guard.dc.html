Why it has to be strictUP rules, 2026
1
Excess is charged twice
Demand above 600 kVA pays the ₹430 demand charge plus the same again as penalty: ₹860 per excess kVA.
2
One spike can reset the sanction
In July 2026 UPPCL raised sanctioned loads in its billing system to each consumer's highest monthly demand of the year. Three straight months over also merges the excess into the sanction.
3
The floor rises with it
You are always billed at least 75% of sanction. A higher sanction raises the winter bill even when the hospital uses less.
4
Your meter has already logged 1,260 kVA
Interim meter SI40022822 recorded that maximum between 23 May and 14 Sep. MVVNL billed 585. A reading like that is enough to trigger rules 1 to 3.
