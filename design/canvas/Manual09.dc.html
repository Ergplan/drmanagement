Demand ceiling procedure

The rule
Never above 570 kVA

ergOS projects each demand interval every minute and sheds non-clinical load before the meter can record more than 570 kVA. 30 kVA stays spare under the 600 kVA sanction for an unplanned scan or chiller start.
